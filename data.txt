[DOC] AMLD_EXCERPT | Article 13
Customer due diligence measures shall include identifying the customer and verifying the customer's identity, and identifying the beneficial owner and taking reasonable measures to verify the beneficial owner's identity.

[DOC] AMLD_EXCERPT | Article 14
Simplified due diligence may be applied where the risk of money laundering or terrorist financing is lower, subject to conditions set by the relevant framework.

[DOC] AMLD_EXCERPT | Article 18
Enhanced due diligence measures shall be applied in higher-risk situations to manage and mitigate those risks.
